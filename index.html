Saipan's Must Try Restuarants

Food is something you always must experience when you travel, so in this article, I would like to give you my top picks on where to done when you are visiting Saipan.

Best Places to Dine

  1. Zoom Cafe
  2. Shirley's Coffee Shop
  3. Mango Six
  4. Ocean Star Restuarant
  5. Panda House Waffles
  6. Mcdonalds

Panda House Waffles🧇🍦🍕🍔| Garapan, Saipan

Panda house serves great pizza, burgurs, and fries which are my personal favorites.
They also serve a number of ice creams, such as blueberry cheesecake, mint chocolate chip,
chocolate etc. They're restaurant is also aesthetic espcially on holidays, where they decorate
their resutrant.

What items I love in their restuarant ❤️

  • Their amazing and delicious pizza
  • The mouth watering burgers that explodes in your tastebuds
  • Delicious and sweet ice cream floats!
